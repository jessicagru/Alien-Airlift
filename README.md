# Alien-Airlift

## Project Option:
Simple Browser Game

## Project Description:
This browser-based space rescue game invites players to guide stranded aliens back to their home planets while navigating through space debris and destroying their extraterrestrial enemies. The experience works with simple movement mechanics, obstacles, score tracking, and level (health) progression. Using their Up/Down W/S keyboard controls, players move their ship across the screen to dodge threats and escort each alien safely to their destination. The game becomes increasingly challenging across three levels.

## Theme Interpretation:
Alien Airlift responds to the theme of Space in that it follows the mission of aliens traveling to their respective planets, in space, while fighting off space hazards and other fictional extraterrestrials.

## Intended User or Player:
Alien Airlift is designed for users aged 7-30+ who enjoy space themes, arcade-style games, and challenges.

## User or Player Goal
The user will be tasked with three missions that lead them on an adventure of dodging hazards, shooting enemies, earning points, and ultimately airlifting their alien passengers to their Home Planet safely.

## Responsive Design
As the screen becomes smaller, the game screen, elements, and text scale down accordingly. The health and score bars stack vertically, rather than being horizontally aligned. The level indicator falls in line with the health bar rather than being in the center, horizontally, between the health and score bars.

## User Testing
Testing indicated that players found the game easy to navigate, with the Level Complete/Failed screens effectively guiding progression. While no major usability issues were identified, both testers recommended adding brief instructional popups at the start of each level to introduce new mechanics as gameplay becomes more challenging. The only notable bug reported by peer-testing was a player bullet occasionally becoming stuck on the screen, while self-testing also revealed issues with the health system carrying damage between levels. 

## Revisions
After testing, I prioritized fixing the bugs of bullets being stuck on the screen and the player automatically losing health on respawn. I also implemented the additional point system of dodging hazards. Lastly, I updated each placeholder element to be a finished and polished visual image.

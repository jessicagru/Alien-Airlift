Rescue stranded aliens across the galaxy while avoiding dangerous obstacles.

⭐ Scoring

Destroy obstacles to collect stars and increase your score.

Successfully dodge an obstacle without getting hit for bonus points.

Lose all hearts and the mission will fail.
